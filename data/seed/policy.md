# Kaveri Precision Components: Procurement Policy v1 (SYNTHETIC)

*Fictional company, synthetic document for the EdgeMemory hackathon build. Effective 1 April 2025.*

## 1. Scope

This policy covers every purchase request (PR) raised against a cost centre at the Hyderabad plant: direct
material, indirect maintenance/repair/operations (MRO) supplies, capital expenditure (capex) and services.
A purchase order (PO) may be issued only after a PR passes the standard checks and is approved.

## 2. Standard checks (every PR)

1. **Budget.** The PR amount must fit within the cost centre's remaining budget for the financial year
   (April to March).
2. **Vendor.** The vendor must be active in the vendor master, its GST registration must be active, and
   every required certificate (for example ISO 9001, IATF 16949, ISO/IEC 17025) must be valid on the PR date.
   Blacklisted vendors may not be used.
3. **Approver.** The PR goes to the approver for its cost centre and amount (the approval matrix): the
   cost-centre head up to ₹2,00,000, the plant head up to ₹10,00,000, the CFO above that. The approver must
   be available on the PR date.
4. **Duplicate.** No PR to the same vendor within 5% of the same amount in the previous 30 days.
5. **Bank details.** Vendor bank details must not have changed in the previous 30 days.
6. **Quotes.** PRs above ₹2,00,000 need at least three quotes attached.

## 3. Approval and delegation

An approver who will be away may register a delegate in the delegation matrix, with a maximum amount and
validity dates. A delegate may approve PRs within that maximum and validity period.

## 4. Payments

Payments are released by Accounts Payable against an approved PO and a matched invoice. Procurement never
releases payments. Changes to vendor bank details are handled by the vendor master team.

## 5. Records

Every PR, approval and exception must be recorded with its reason. Internal audit may review any PR.

## Memo effective 1 July 2026 (amends §3)

For capex requests, when the approver is on leave, the delegate may approve only with Finance Controller
co-sign (ADD_CONTROLLER_SIGNOFF).
